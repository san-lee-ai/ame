RCJ FAQ

Questions about
Rural Care Journey

What is Rural Care Journey (RCJ)?

Rural Care Journey (RCJ) is AME Mobile’s research and intelligence platform for the Rural Health Transformation Program (RHTP). It brings together rural health funding opportunities, state programs, public documents, vendors, and community updates in one place.

What is the Rural Health Transformation Program (RHTP)?

The Rural Health Transformation Program is a $50 billion federal investment, administered by CMS, to strengthen sustainable access to care in rural America. Funding flows from CMS to states, which run their own programs and release funding opportunities to rural providers and partners. RCJ tracks that state-level activity across all 50 states — see how RHTP funding flows from CMS to communities.

Where can I find RHTP grants, deadlines, and awards?

Rural Care Journey lists open RHTP grants and opportunities, RHTP deadlines closing soon, RHTP awards and funded projects, and RHTP activity by state, each linked to the underlying public record.

Who is Rural Care Journey for?

RCJ is built for grant applicants, rural hospitals and clinics, vendors, researchers, implementation partners, and rural communities who need a practical starting point for rural health transformation activity.

How often is Rural Care Journey updated?

RCJ is updated daily from public sources, including state Flex Program offices, federal agencies, funding announcements, awards, and published program documents.

Is Rural Care Journey affiliated with HRSA or CMS?

No. Rural Care Journey is an independent research tool built by AME Mobile. It is not affiliated with HRSA or CMS and is intended for research use.
