Why Rural Care Journey

Rural health information
in one place.

“Right time. Right help. Closer to home.”

Rural Care Journey · A research tool by AME Mobile

Rural Care Journey helps people make sense of a fast-moving rural health landscape: what states are planning, where funding is available, which organizations are involved, and what is changing next.

Built by AME Mobile, RCJ turns public-source activity into a practical starting point for applicants, vendors, researchers, implementation partners, and rural communities.

The Problem RCJ Solves

Rural health moves fast.
Finding the signal is hard.

01Important opportunities are spread across dozens of state and federal sources.
02Program documents are difficult to compare, search, and turn into next steps.
03Potential partners need a clearer way to find one another and move from interest to action.

Rural Care Journey addresses this by bringing public-source intelligence, practical tools, and a growing community into one navigable place — so the people doing the work can spend less time searching and more time moving forward.
